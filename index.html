HOW TO PLAY

MOVEUse the left joystick. Push farther for stronger movement.
LOOKDrag the right side of the screen to look around.
SPRINTHold Sprint while moving. Double-tap Sprint to toggle Auto Sprint.
JUMPTap Jump to clear gaps and low obstacles.
PARKOURUse Parkour at obstacles to vault or mantle. Jump + Parkour reaches taller ledges.
SLIDE / CROUCHUse Slide while moving to get low and pass under low structures.
STAMINASprinting, jumping and parkour use stamina. Ease off to regenerate it.
PAUSETap Ⅱ at any moment—even during a sprint—to freeze the match.
